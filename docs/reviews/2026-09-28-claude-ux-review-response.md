# Codex response to Claude's UX review — 2026-09-28

Reviewed Claude's `5fdec77` against the unchanged v0.19.0 application, alongside the engineering response. This is an assessment and backlog update, not a redesign or policy change. Agree refers to the finding, not blanket acceptance of every suggested remedy.

Evidence: source inspection of `public/index.html`, `public/app.js`, `public/style.css` and the check workflow; a local disposable UI fixture with no provider calls; and visual inspection of the supplied prototype. This was not a repeat of Claude's complete Linux journey, a live deployment test, or a full accessibility audit. The fixture cannot establish every backend outcome. Implementation acceptance remains outstanding.

## Prototype assessment

The conversation-first layout, unified composer and focused review surface are useful direction. Keep the prototype as a discussion artifact; do not ship its script. It uses `innerHTML`, external Google Fonts and canned state transitions, unlike the product's security and data requirements. Its sample content does not resolve the non-npm check prerequisite.

Calculated normal-text contrast for prototype examples is about 3.32:1 for `#8b8984` on `#faf9f7`, and 3.90:1 for white on `#c96442`. Those combinations need adjustment where used for ordinary text: [WCAG contrast guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html) specifies 4.5:1 for ordinary text and 3:1 for large text. Inspect actual roles, sizes and states rather than approving a palette from a screenshot.
